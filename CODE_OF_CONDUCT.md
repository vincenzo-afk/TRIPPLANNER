# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in TRIPPLANNER a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include showing empathy, being respectful of differing viewpoints, giving and accepting constructive feedback, and taking responsibility for mistakes. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, public or private abuse, discrimination, sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and publishing another person’s private information without permission.

## Enforcement responsibilities

The project maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code of conduct and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this code. The maintainer may also take other actions when appropriate.

## Scope

This code applies within project spaces and whenever an individual is representing the project or its community in public spaces. Representation includes using an official project email address, posting through an official account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer through the [vincenzo-afk GitHub profile](https://github.com/vincenzo-afk). Reports will be reviewed and handled as appropriate to the circumstances.
